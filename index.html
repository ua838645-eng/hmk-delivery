Frequently Asked Questions

Are your deliveries fully insured in Canada?

Yes. HMK Trading Inc. carries comprehensive cargo insurance up to $50,000 CAD per trip across Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, and Quebec.

How does the 4-Digit Delivery PIN work?

When your booking is confirmed, a unique 4-digit PIN is generated. Your driver will ask for this PIN upon arrival to verify delivery and trigger escrow payment release.

Can I get a CRA-compliant tax receipt?

Absoutely. Click on "CRA Tax Receipt" on any trip page or invoice modal to view and print official receipts showing our CRA Business Number (BN 84920 1948 RT0001).

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Interac e-Transfer, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, HMK CAD Wallet balances, and Corporate Net-30 billing for approved business accounts.
