Common questions

What is the 4-digit PIN?

You get it when you book. Give it to the driver only when your cargo arrives; the driver enters it to complete the delivery.

Can I cancel?

Yes, until the cargo is in transit. Open My Orders and press Cancel Order. Wallet payments are returned to your wallet.

How is tax calculated?

By the hub you pick: HST 13% (Ontario), 12% (British Columbia), GST 5% (Alberta), 14.975% (Quebec).

Can I schedule a pickup?

Yes. In the booking form choose "Schedule for later" and pick a date and time.

Problem with a delivery?

After delivery, open the order in My Orders, describe the problem and press Report Issue.
